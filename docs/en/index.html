Infrastructure projects

Networks, datacentres and connectivity delivered from design to operation

Three representative deployments from recent years, with the scope executed and the outcome delivered. The diagrams below describe the topology actually deployed.

Clinical laboratory · 3 months

Enterprise Wi-Fi and segmentation — BIONALISE

  • ProblemFlat network with no separation between departments and unreliable Wi-Fi coverage in the lab.
  • SolutionMikroTik firewall, managed core switch, three VLANs (admin, laboratory, guest) and enterprise access points.
  • OutcomeGuest traffic isolated from clinical systems, full coverage and physically organised racks.
  • MikroTik
  • VLAN
  • Enterprise Wi-Fi
  • Firewall

Clinical laboratory · connectivity

Site-to-site VPN — ITALAB

  • ProblemSeparate units needing to share the sample-collection and reporting system securely.
  • SolutionEncrypted VPN tunnel between MikroTik routers, routing between local networks and link monitoring.
  • OutcomeContinuous operation across units, with no system exposed to the public internet.
  • VPN
  • MikroTik
  • Routing
  • Security

Dairy industry · 4 months

Complete datacentre — Laticínios Vila Nova

  • ProblemAn industrial site with no formal infrastructure: scattered equipment, improvised cabling, no CCTV or integrated telephony.
  • SolutionOrganised 24U rack, Furukawa-certified cabling, file server, CCTV and IP telephony integrated.
  • OutcomeDocumented, maintainable infrastructure with labelled network points across every department.
  • Datacentre
  • Furukawa
  • CCTV
  • IP telephony
